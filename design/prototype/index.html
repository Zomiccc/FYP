What the law says, in simple words

✓ Every point has a source

Based on the rent law sources we found, your landlord has to follow the proper legal process before a tenant can be removed S1. A phone call alone is usually not treated as a proper notice S2. Since your written agreement runs until March 2027, its terms also matter S3.

What you can do now: keep paying rent and keep the receipts, ask the landlord to put any notice in writing, and speak to a property lawyer if the pressure continues.
